# Ludo Effekseer resources

- `runtime/`: EffekseerForWebGL 1.80.5 JavaScript/WASM runtime.
- `vs/vs-burst.efkefc`: official `ToonHit.efkefc` retained as the editable source reference.
- `vs/vs-burst-v1806.efkefc`: the effect re-saved by the locally installed Effekseer 1.80.6 and loaded by Ludo.
- `vs/Parts/`: resources referenced by the editable effect.

The runtime and starting sample come from the official Effekseer projects and
are distributed under the adjacent MIT license. The Ludo page overlays its own
`VS` lettering so the text stays sharp at every device resolution.
